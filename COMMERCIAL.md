# Selling OpenStellarFirm

OpenStellarFirm is free to use, change and share. Selling it is not included
in the licence. This page says where the line is.

This page is a plain summary. The [LICENSE](LICENSE) file is what counts.

## What you can do with no licence from us

- Use the app, at home and at work, in a company of any size.
- Read the code, change it, and run your changed version.
- Share the app or your changed version with other people, at no charge, under
  the same licence, with the licence and notices kept.
- Make personas and assistants with it and share or use them as you like. What
  you make with the app is yours.
- Get paid for your own work that happens to use the app, such as writing,
  research or code you did with an assistant's help.

## What needs a commercial licence

Anything where people pay you, in money or in kind, for something whose value
comes entirely or mostly from this software. For example:

- Selling the app or a renamed copy of it, as a download, a licence key or a
  subscription.
- Offering it as a hosted or managed service.
- Selling it bundled in a product, a device or a paid plan.
- Selling installation, hosting or support for it as a business.

If you are not sure which side you are on, ask before you sell.

## How to get one

Write to Astro Code L.P. through [stellarfirm.ai](https://stellarfirm.ai) and
say what you want to sell and where.

## Names and marks

A commercial licence, like the public one, does not include the names
"StellarFirm" and "OpenStellarFirm", the logos or the pet characters, unless
it says so in writing.
