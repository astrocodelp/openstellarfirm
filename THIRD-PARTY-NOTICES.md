The reader that opens PDFs and reads pictures on this computer is built from
PDF.js and Tesseract.js, used unchanged and bundled into the app's own files.
The licence texts of PDF.js's decoders ship beside them in the app, in
`reader/cmaps/LICENSE` and `reader/wasm/LICENSE_*`. A language you download
is the Tesseract project's own trained data, checked against its SHA-256
before it is kept.

The fonts are used unchanged. The SIL Open Font License is at
https://openfontlicense.org.

Build tools (esbuild, Vite, TypeScript, Vitest, Biome, electron-builder) are
used to make the app and are not part of it.
