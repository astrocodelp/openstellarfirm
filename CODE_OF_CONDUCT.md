# Code of Conduct

This is a place to build something useful together. These rules apply in the
repository, in Discussions, and anywhere else the project meets.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new, and many are not developers.
- Talk about the work, not the person.
- Say what you mean plainly, and assume the other person means well.
- Give reasons with a "no", and take a "no" well.
- Give credit for other people's work.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment, or attacks on who someone is.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam, and promotion that has nothing to do with the project.
- Sharing personas or code meant to deceive, harm or impersonate a real
  person.

## What happens

A maintainer may hide or remove a comment, close a thread, or, for repeated or
serious cases, block an account from the project. A maintainer explains the
reason when they act.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers privately through the repository's
[Security tab](https://github.com/astrocodelp/worker-bot-open-source/security)
("Report a vulnerability" reaches the maintainers only), and say that it is a
conduct report. We keep the reporter's name private.
